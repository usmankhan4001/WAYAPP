# Getting Started with WAYAPP

Welcome to WAYAPP! This guide is written for non-developers. We'll show you how to connect your WhatsApp Business account, manage contacts, and send your first campaign.

## 1. Connect WhatsApp Business
1. Go to the **Settings** page in WAYAPP.
2. Follow the link to log into your Meta/Facebook Developer account.
3. Copy your **WhatsApp Phone Number ID** and **Access Token**.
4. Paste them into the WAYAPP settings and click **Save**.
5. *Tip:* Make sure your number is verified by Meta to send messages without limits!

## 2. Sync Your Templates
Templates are pre-approved messages you can send to start conversations.
1. Create templates in your Meta Business Manager.
2. In WAYAPP, go to the **Templates** tab.
3. Click **Sync Templates**. WAYAPP will automatically download all your approved templates from Meta.

## 3. Import Contacts
1. Go to the **Contacts** tab.
2. Click **Import**.
3. Upload a CSV file with your contacts. Make sure it has a column named `phone` (with country codes, e.g., `1234567890`) and `name`.
4. WAYAPP will import them instantly.

## 4. Send a Campaign
1. Go to **Broadcasts** and click **New Broadcast**.
2. Select the audience (all contacts, or specific tags).
3. Choose the template you synced earlier.
4. Review the estimated audience size and click **Send**.
5. Head over to the **Dashboard** to see live delivery and read rates!
