# 💼 Sales Suite, AI Co-Pilot & Pipeline CRM Guide

The Sales Suite in WAYAPP is specifically designed for non-technical sales agents, account executives, and customer support representatives who want a fast, familiar **WhatsApp Web-style interface** combined with powerful CRM tools.

---

## ⚡ 1. AI Sales Co-Pilot

The AI Co-Pilot toolbar is integrated directly above the live chat input box. It works with any configured LLM (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, OpenRouter) and includes automatic intelligent fallbacks if no external API key is present.

### Co-Pilot Features:
1. **⚡ Suggest Reply**:
   * Reads the last 5 messages of the conversation.
   * Generates 2 distinct, persuasive sales reply options (e.g. Option A: Direct price quote, Option B: Consultation invite).
   * Clicking a suggestion inserts it immediately into the draft input.
2. **🪄 Polish Tone**:
   * Takes rough bullet points or informal text in the input box and rewrites it into a professional, courteous, and engaging WhatsApp response.
3. **🌐 Instant Translation**:
   * Translates drafted text into Arabic, Urdu, French, Spanish, German, or Portuguese with one click.
4. **📝 Summarize History**:
   * Analyzes the complete chat history and generates a 3-bullet handover summary (**Customer Intent**, **Shared Information**, **Next Step Required**) when reassigning a deal to another sales representative.

## 💬 2. Canned Snippets & Action Bar

### Triggering Snippets with `/`
Typing `/` in the chat input opens an instant search popup displaying team shortcuts:
* `/pricing` $\rightarrow$ Inserts standard enterprise pricing sheet and packages.
* `/brochure` $\rightarrow$ Inserts company product catalog and overview PDF link.
* `/discount` $\rightarrow$ Inserts limited-time promotional discount code.
* `/demo` $\rightarrow$ Inserts Calendly consultation booking link.
* `/bank-details` $\rightarrow$ Inserts official wire transfer and IBAN instructions.
* `/location` $\rightarrow$ Inserts office Google Maps location and working hours.

### 1-Click Action Bar:
* 💳 **Payment / Invoice Link**: Opens a modal to input customer amount, description, and currency $\rightarrow$ generates a payment message.
* 📦 **Product Catalog**: Dispatches the official business product catalog link.
* 📅 **Book Consultation**: Dispatches a meeting booking link.

## 📊 3. In-Chat Lead CRM Panel

The right-hand side panel inside the live chat window enables agents to manage customer metadata without switching tools:
* **Lead Stage Selector**: Update customer deal stage in real time (`New Lead` $\rightarrow$ `Contacted` $\rightarrow$ `Qualified` $\rightarrow$ `Proposal Sent` $\rightarrow$ `Deal Won` $\rightarrow$ `Deal Lost`).
* **Estimated Deal Value**: Inline currency editor (e.g. `$25,000`).
* **Company & City**: Customer business details.
* **Color-Coded Tags**: Add or remove tags like `VIP Corporate`, `High Intent`, `Retail`.
* **Yellow Sticky Notes Tab**: Private internal notes for sales reps and managers (stored securely in the database and never transmitted over WhatsApp).
* **Activity Audit Timeline**: Complete chronological history of lead capture events, stage changes, and agent assignments.

---

## 📈 4. Visual Sales Pipeline Kanban Board

Located at `/contacts`, the Sales Pipeline Kanban Board provides managers and sales teams with a birds-eye view of all ongoing opportunities.

### Features:
* **Dual View Switcher**: Toggle between standard **Table View** and **Kanban Pipeline Board**.
* **6 Visual Columns**:
  1. 🟦 `New Lead`
  2. 🟨 `Contacted`
  3. 🟪 `Qualified`
  4. 🟦 `Proposal Sent`
  5. 🟩 `Deal Won`
  6. 🟥 `Deal Lost`
* **Real-Time Revenue Ribbon**:
  * Total Contacts Count
  * Active Pipeline Deals
  * Weighted Pipeline Forecast ($)
  * Closed Won Revenue ($)
* **1-Click Card Actions**: Move deals between stages, edit values, view tags, or click **Chat** to jump directly into the live conversation.
